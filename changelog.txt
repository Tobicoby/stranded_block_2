Version: NEXT
  Features:
    - Space Age: extraction recipes for every planet. Stranded Block only covered Nauvis, so a
      planet without ore patches (resources switched off in the map generator, or a scenario
      that places none) had no renewable source of its own resources. At load time each
      planet now gets a recipe per resource that generates there, made from the fluid an
      offshore pump yields there (lava, heavy oil, ammoniacal solution, water). This also
      covers planets and Nauvis ores added by other mods.
    - The new recipes craft in chemical plants and cryogenic plants, unlock with the planet's
      discovery technology and share the mining productivity research.
    - Technologies researched by mining a resource patch can also be researched by extracting
      that resource. Calcite processing needs a calcite patch to be mined, so without this a
      Vulcanus without patches is a dead end even with the new recipes. Mining still works as
      before. A technology can only have one trigger, so a hidden twin technology carries the
      extraction trigger and completing either one completes the other. Triggers that mine
      rocks, ruins or plants are left alone, as those still generate.
    - Advanced Extraction technologies, one per planet. Advanced Water Smelting
      lets Nauvis skip the furnace, but the other planets had no equivalent mid-game step.
      Where a planet's resource is smelted or recycled on its own (stone on Gleba, scrap on
      Fulgora, and whatever other mods add), a recipe now makes the products of that step
      directly from the planet's fluid. Each technology needs Advanced Water Smelting, the
      planet's discovery, and space science plus the planet's own science pack. The recipes
      share the water smelting productivity research.
  Changes:
    - Space Age: every extraction and water smelting recipe only works on the planet whose
      resource it makes, so Nauvis ores can no longer be made from water on Gleba.
